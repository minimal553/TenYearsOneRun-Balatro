## 运势改变条件，出牌仍由你决定

游戏中，本命档与当前大运档共同决定门槛和运签。最顺组合能拿到灵魂，但你仍需要留出小丑槽、选择使用时机，再围绕得到的传奇构筑。

![上上组合获得原版灵魂的实机效果](https://github.com/minimal553/TenYearsOneRun-Balatro/raw/refs/heads/main/docs/images/soul-reward.png)

| 档位 | 本版运签奖励 |
|---|---|
| 1 | 原版灵魂：使用后生成传奇小丑，需空余小丑槽 |
| 2、3 | 一张随机幻灵 |
| 4、5 | 两张随机塔罗 |
| 6 | 两张倍率增强牌或两张奖励筹码增强牌，各50% |
| 7、8 | 一张随机塔罗或一张随机星球，各50% |
| 9 | 冥王星，提升高牌等级 |

不输入生日也能试玩：“九宫预设”提供九种固定档挑战。想体验命盘与大运共同塑造牌局，则选择“输入生日”。

## 下载，开始你的这一局

在本页 **Assets** 中下载 **`TenYearsNineGrid-0.7.0-20260923-111844.zip`**（约3.4 MB）。另附SHA 256文件供核对。

1. 自行安装Balatro、Lovely和Steamodded。
2. 关闭游戏，把ZIP里的`TenYearsNineGrid`文件夹放入`%APPDATA%/Balatro/Mods`。
3. 新开一局，选择青绿朱砂命盘牌背的**「十年一局·命局牌组」→「输入生日」**。

旧存档保留旧档次与旧奖励；新表需要新开局。出生输入不上传；四柱与大运快照仅为继续游戏而保存在本地存档。

## Bazi × Balatro — play through the turns of fate

Your birth chart gives the run its opening Joker. Each new ante advances the decade table and can change the target and reward. Build around your pattern, make the most of favorable conditions, and adapt when the next stage asks more of you.

This unofficial mod includes 26 chart-pattern Jokers,12 optional combinations,8 decade stages and 9 reward tiers. Birthday mode is the core experience; fixed presets are a quick, birthday-free way to try it.

Download the packaged ZIP under **Assets**, copy its mod folder into your Mods directory, and start a new run. The UI is currently Simplified Chinese; the [full English guide](https://github.com/minimal553/TenYearsOneRun-Balatro/blob/main/README.en.md) explains every card and mechanic. The experimental classifier does not predict real-world outcomes.
